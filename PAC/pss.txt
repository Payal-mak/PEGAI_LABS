## Question 1 — Generative AI-Powered Research Assistant Website

A postgraduate engineering student has identified a research problem and plans to publish a research paper in a reputed journal. However, the student faces difficulties in conducting literature surveys, identifying research gaps, selecting appropriate methodologies, comparing existing research works, managing citations, summarizing research papers, and preparing a high-quality manuscript.

To assist researchers, develop a **Generative AI-powered Research Assistant Website** that provides end-to-end support throughout the research lifecycle.

The website should allow users to:

* Perform literature surveys.
* Generate research keywords and search queries.
* Retrieve and summarize relevant research papers.
* Identify research gaps.
* Formulate research objectives and research questions.
* Recommend suitable datasets, algorithms, experimental methodologies, and evaluation metrics.
* Generate comparative analysis tables.
* Prepare citations in different formats.
* Draft various sections of a research paper.
* Maintain research history.

Design comprehensive prompt workflows for every research stage by applying appropriate **Prompt Engineering techniques**, such as:

* Role prompting
* Structured prompting
* Few-shot prompting
* Template prompting
* Prompt chaining

Implement prompt refinement by progressively improving prompts to enhance the accuracy, completeness, relevance, and quality of AI-generated outputs.

Integrate suitable Generative AI APIs into the website and design an intuitive user interface that enables seamless interaction between the user and the AI system.

Validate the generated information using trusted scholarly databases and official research repositories. Evaluate the generated outputs for:

* Factual correctness
* Technical accuracy
* Consistency
* Citation quality
* Completeness
* Hallucinations
* Plagiarism

Finally, recommend suitable strategies for:

* Responsible AI usage
* Research ethics
* Data privacy
* Transparency
* Future enhancements

These strategies should improve the overall effectiveness and reliability of the developed research assistant.
